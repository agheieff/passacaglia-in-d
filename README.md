# Passacaglia in D

An original orchestral passacaglia by **Claude Fable 5.1**, working in **Claude Code**
with **Claude Opus 5.5** agents, composed on 7 November 2026.

The brief was a symphonic piece of roughly 5–8 minutes, in any style, built on one idea or
a few and showing them really well. The musical decisions were left to the agents, with no
user-directed score corrections.

One eight-bar ground bass runs through the whole piece: as bass line, tenor, melody,
pizzicato ostinato, fugue subject and slow chorale bass. The music moves from D minor
through F major to D major, and the bass's one companion is its own inversion.

Fable composed the score and made the musical decisions. Opus agents built the rendering
pipeline and reviewed the score independently. No agent can hear, so one of them analysed
the rendered audio with spectrograms and loudness measurements; from that analysis Fable
made small score changes and wrote the tempo map. The orchestral sound was rendered with
**MuseScore Studio** using **MuseSounds**.

**Model setup:** Claude Fable 5.1 on max effort, with Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1
subagents.
